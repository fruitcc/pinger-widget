# Privacy Policy — Pinger

*Effective July 2, 2026*

Pinger ("the app"), published by Fruit Cloud Co., LLC, does not collect,
store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Period.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts,
  and no third-party SDKs.
- **Network use.** The app's only network activity is sending small ICMP echo
  ("ping") packets to the destination hosts you configure (for example
  `8.8.8.8`), in order to measure your connection's latency and packet loss.
  Nothing about you or your device is included in these packets, and no
  results ever leave your Mac.
- **Local storage.** Your settings (destinations, thresholds, intervals) are
  stored locally on your Mac in the app's sandboxed preferences and are never
  transmitted anywhere.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/fruitcc/pinger-widget or contact the developer.
